About Me

I am a certified Human Resources professional with over four years of progressive experience in HR operations and analytics. My expertise spans the full spectrum of talent management—including onboarding and offboarding, HRIS administration, HR analytics, and dashboard development. I bring a strong blend of technical proficiency and strategic HR insight, with a particular passion for HR analytics and leveraging data-driven insights to empower a more productive and engaged workforce. I have successfully led employee engagement initiatives, optimized onboarding processes, and built analytical reporting systems to inform organizational decision-making.
